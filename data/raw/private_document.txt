Yasir Siddiqui was born in Sukkur in 1960. He studied at Ashdown Institute of Technology and later at Riverbend College of Science. He received the Alderwick Medal for Theoretical Physics in 2007. Colleagues usually referred to him simply as "Dr. Siddiqui". In 1995, Dr. Siddiqui joined Westmere Institute of Technology. Yasir Siddiqui's doctoral advisor was Bushra Awan. She later moved to Marlow Institute of Technology. Despite a persistent rumour, he never studied at Alderwick University.
